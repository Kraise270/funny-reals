# Privacy Policy — Funny_reals

**Last updated: November 20, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Funny_reals is a video processing and content creation application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

## 2. Information Processing

The application may process:

- Video and audio files selected by the user.
- Transcriptions generated from audio.
- Text and metadata used to identify interesting video moments.
- TikTok account authorization information when TikTok integration is enabled.

## 3. Local Processing

Video processing, transcription, and AI analysis may be performed locally on the user's computer using FFmpeg, Faster-Whisper, and Ollama.

## 4. Third-Party Services

When integrations are enabled, information may be exchanged with third-party services, including TikTok, Twitch, and YouTube.

Their own privacy policies apply to information processed by those services.

## 5. Data Storage

Video files and temporary processing data may be stored locally on the user's computer.

The application may remove temporary files after processing according to its cleanup settings.

## 6. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Content is transmitted to third-party services when the user requests or enables related functionality.

## 7. Security

Users are responsible for protecting their computers, credentials, and API tokens.

## 8. User Rights

Users may stop using the application and remove locally stored application data.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the service operator through the contact information provided with the application.
